What belongs in a business buyer profile?

A useful buyer profile states the industries and locations you will consider, total transaction range, available equity, financing stage, desired owner role, relevant operating experience, income needs, timing, decision makers, and the risks you will or will not accept. It should be specific enough to screen opportunities without exposing unnecessary sensitive information.

Eight criteria

Write the profile before contacting sellers

Industry and model

List preferred sectors, customer types, operating models, licenses, and categories you will not pursue.

Geography

Define travel radius, relocation limits, remote-management expectations, and property preferences.

Transaction range

Separate total price from available equity, fees, improvements, inventory adjustments, and working capital.

Financing stage

State whether you are researching, prequalified, working with a lender, or able to evaluate seller financing.

Operating role

Clarify full-time, manager-run, strategic, family-operated, or add-on expectations.

Experience

Connect management, sales, technical, regulated, financial, or industry experience to the target.

Risk boundaries

Set limits for owner dependence, concentration, turnaround work, lease exposure, capital needs, and seasonality.

Timing and team

Identify decision makers, advisers, target closing window, and any dependency on another transaction.
