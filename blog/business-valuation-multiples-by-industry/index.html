Quick answer

Business Valuation Multiples by Industry

Business valuation multiples vary by industry, size, earnings definition, growth, concentration, owner dependence, systems, capital needs and transaction terms. Use sourced market evidence with normalized SDE or EBITDA, apply a range instead of one point estimate, and reconcile the result to assets, debt, working capital and the proposed deal structure.

Choose the right earnings base

  • SDE is often used for smaller owner-operated businesses; EBITDA is more common when replacement management and larger-company economics matter.
  • Reconcile every adjustment to source records and distinguish truly nonrecurring items from ordinary costs.
  • Do not compare a revenue multiple with an earnings multiple or mix data from different size bands without explanation.

What moves a company within a range?

  • Recurring revenue, documented growth, low concentration, transferable systems and management depth can support stronger positioning.
  • Owner dependence, volatile margins, customer loss, weak records, deferred capital expenditures and lease risk can reduce it.
  • Cash, debt, working capital, inventory and real estate may be handled outside the headline multiple.

Use industry data carefully

  • Record the source date, transaction type, size range, geography, earnings definition and whether values are asking prices or closed transactions.
  • A broad industry median may not represent a specific company.
  • Use multiple methods and explain why the selected evidence is comparable.

Turn a range into a decision

  • Calculate low, midpoint and high cases with the same normalized earnings base.
  • Stress-test customer loss, margin changes, owner replacement compensation and capital needs.
  • Compare enterprise value with the equity and proceeds outcomes after debt and transaction-specific adjustments.

Decision checklist

VerifyTrace important assumptions to financial, legal and operating evidence.
ModelTest price, structure, financing, working capital and downside cases together.
DocumentMake terms, owners, deadlines and unresolved items explicit.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good EBITDA multiple?

A multiple is only meaningful relative to company size, industry, risk, growth, earnings quality and deal terms. A generic number should not substitute for comparable evidence.

Are SDE and EBITDA interchangeable?

No. They use different normalization conventions and often apply to different buyer/operator assumptions.

Do valuation multiples include inventory?

Treatment varies. The LOI and purchase agreement should state what is included, excluded or adjusted at closing.

Can a calculator determine market value?

A calculator can organize assumptions and show sensitivity, but it does not replace market evidence, diligence or qualified valuation advice.
