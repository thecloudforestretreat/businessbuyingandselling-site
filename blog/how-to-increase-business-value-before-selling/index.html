Increase Earnings Before the Sale

Many businesses are valued using a multiple of earnings. That means each dollar of improved profit can create outsized value when multiplied in the sale price.

  • Raise pricing where justified
  • Remove wasteful expenses
  • Improve gross margins
  • Exit low-value customers
  • Focus on profitable channels
  • Normalize discretionary spend

For valuation context, read What Your Business Is Really Worth Before You Sell.

Reduce Buyer Risk

Risk lowers multiples. If buyers see uncertainty, they usually reduce price, demand seller financing, or walk away.

Clean FinancialsAccurate and organized reporting.
Tax ComplianceRemove surprises.
Customer DiversityReduce concentration risk.
ContractsDocument suppliers and clients.

Make the Business Easier to Transfer

A business that only works because of the owner often receives lower offers. Buyers want a company that can run after transition.

The easier a business is to inherit, the more valuable it often becomes.

Increase transferability by documenting systems, training managers, delegating key relationships, and building repeatable operations.

How Early Should You Start?

Frequently Asked Questions

Often yes, especially through margin improvement, cleaner books, and risk reduction.

What raises valuation most?

Higher transferable earnings and lower risk usually matter most.

Does revenue growth alone increase value?

Not always. Profit quality matters more than revenue alone.

Should I wait until listing to improve things?

No. Improvements are strongest when made before market launch.
