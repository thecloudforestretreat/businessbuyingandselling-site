How to use the result

Planning estimate, not a conclusion

Start with reported EBITDA, add only documented nonrecurring or owner-specific expenses, and subtract replacement compensation or recurring costs a buyer will incur. The result is a planning estimate. Each adjustment should be supported, consistently classified and reviewed during diligence.

  • Change one assumption at a time and compare scenarios.
  • Keep supporting records for every adjustment or term.
  • Confirm lending, tax, accounting and legal treatment with qualified professionals.

What this calculator includes

  • Inputs are visible and editable; no hidden benchmark is presented as fact.
  • Results update in the browser and are not transmitted by this calculator.
  • The model shows relationships and sensitivity, not eligibility, market value or enforceable terms.

Primary sources and methodology

  • SEC guidance on non-GAAP financial measures
  • IRS Form 8594 and instructions
