How we think

Transaction readiness

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Business Buying and Selling

What does Business Buying and Selling help with?

We help buyers explore businesses for sale and help owners understand the process of preparing, valuing, and selling a business.

Do you work with both buyers and sellers?

Yes. The platform is built for both sides, with listings, buyer education, seller resources, and contact paths for next-step conversations.

Why is confidentiality important?

Confidentiality helps protect employees, customers, vendors, sellers, and buyers while serious conversations and diligence are being evaluated.
