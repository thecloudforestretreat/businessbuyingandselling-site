How can a buyer finance a business acquisition?

A buyer may combine personal equity with an SBA-backed loan, conventional bank debt, seller financing, investor equity, or another supported source. The workable mix depends on borrower eligibility, verified business cash flow, collateral and guarantees, deal structure, lender policy, current program rules, and enough remaining liquidity for transaction costs and post-closing operations.

Borrower readiness

Credit history, liquidity, personal financial information, relevant experience, outside obligations, equity source, and management plan.

Business evidence

Tax returns, interim financials, normalized earnings, debt schedule, assets, contracts, lease, licenses, and credible projections.

Transaction structure

Price allocation, asset or equity structure, seller note, working capital, inventory, real estate, closing costs, and transition terms.
