The four-stage access process

Identify the opportunity

Use the anonymous BBAS reference and share your industry, price range, experience, capital and timing.

Complete qualification

We may confirm identity, buyer fit, financing stage and the decision makers who will receive information.

Accept confidentiality terms

An NDA must be fully executed and approved before protected listing details are released.

Use secure access

Authorized buyers receive a time-limited link to only the listings approved for them.

What remains protected

Identity and location

Business name, exact address, identifiable landmarks, owner identity and direct contact details.

Actual images

Exterior, interior, signage, branded equipment and other photographs that could reveal the business.

Source documents

Marketing packages, financial statements, leases, property records, licenses and diligence files.

Access history

Private viewing is tied to the approved buyer, expires automatically and may be revoked.

An NDA does not verify a listing, guarantee seller approval, reserve an opportunity or replace independent legal, accounting, tax, valuation, licensing, environmental or financing review.

Buyer conduct

Do not contact a business, owner, employee, customer, supplier, landlord or neighbor you believe you have identified. Do not forward, reproduce or publish private information. Use the secure access link only for the approved purpose and participants.
