Why we exist

Buying or selling a small business should not feel like walking into a fog.

Too many listings hide the basics, too many conversations start without alignment, and too many people waste time. We are building a better experience for everyday owners and first-time buyers: clearer information, practical education, and a smoother path from discovery to action.

Clarity

Consistent listing structure and simple metrics that make sense at a glance.

Confidence

Guides, checklists, and examples so you can evaluate without guessing.

Momentum

Cleaner handoffs and better next steps when both sides are serious.
