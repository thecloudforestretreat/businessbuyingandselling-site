Why we exist

Buying or selling a small business should not feel like walking into a fog. Too many listings lack the basics. Too many conversations start without alignment. Too many people waste time.

We are building a better experience for everyday owners and first-time buyers: clearer information, practical education, and a smoother path from discovery to action.
