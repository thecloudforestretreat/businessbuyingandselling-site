Why we exist

Buying or selling a small business should not feel like walking into a fog. Too many listings lack the basics, too many conversations start without alignment, and too many people waste time. Our goal is to make the process understandable for everyday owners and first-time buyers.

Cleaner listings

Standard fields that matter: revenue, cash flow, margin, lease, and what is included in the sale.

Buyer readiness

Guides and checklists that help you move from interest to action without guesswork.

Owner confidence

Plain-language steps that make selling feel possible even if you have never done it before.
