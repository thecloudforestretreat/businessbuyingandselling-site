Direct answer

What determines what a small business is worth?

A small business value is generally supported by normalized earnings, an appropriate market multiple or valuation method, transferable assets and systems, expected capital needs, working capital, growth quality, concentration, owner dependence, and deal structure. An asking price or calculator result alone is not an independent valuation.

Evidence package

Prepare the records a buyer will test

Financial history

Organize tax returns, profit-and-loss statements, balance sheets, bank activity, debt, payroll, inventory, and capital expenditures for consistent periods.

Normalized earnings

Reconcile each proposed owner, discretionary, nonrecurring, or non-operating adjustment to source records. Unsupported add-backs weaken credibility.

Transferability

Document customer and supplier concentration, contracts, employees, licenses, systems, leases, intellectual property, and the owner's actual role.

Evidence boundary: this page and the calculator provide preparation and educational estimates. A formal conclusion may require a qualified valuation professional, current market data, and deal-specific review.

Frequently asked questions

Business valuation questions

Is a calculator result the value of my business?

No. It is a scenario based on the inputs and assumptions selected. A supportable conclusion requires verified facts, an appropriate method, current evidence, and professional judgment where warranted.

What weakens a valuation?

Unsupported add-backs, inconsistent records, owner dependence, concentration, short leases, deferred maintenance, uncertain licenses, declining margins, and unplanned working-capital or capital needs can reduce confidence or value.

Should I value the business before going to market?

Yes. Early work helps identify evidence gaps, value drivers, likely buyer questions, and whether preparation could improve transferability before confidential marketing begins.

Prepare before discussing price.

Share the business type, ownership role, revenue range, earnings basis, desired timing, and records currently available. Do not send sensitive documents through an initial contact form.
