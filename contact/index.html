Frequently asked questions

Common contact questions

Can I contact you about buying a business?

Yes. Buyers can reach out with questions about listings, business fit, financing, diligence, or next steps.

Can I contact you about selling my business?

Yes. Owners can reach out about valuation, timing, confidentiality, buyer readiness, and preparing for a future sale.

What should I include in my message?

Include whether you are buying or selling, your timing, business type, location, budget, and the specific question or listing you want help with.
