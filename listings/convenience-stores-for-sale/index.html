What should buyers compare?

When comparing convenience stores for sale, verify sales by category, gross margins, fuel economics where applicable, lottery contribution, shrink, payroll, inventory, licenses, supplier and brand agreements, lease or real-estate terms, equipment condition, environmental exposure, working capital, and the owner's actual operating role.
