What should buyers verify before buying a gas station?

Verify fuel gallons and margin by period, inside sales and category margins, brand and supply agreements, equipment ownership, tanks and environmental records, licenses, inventory, payroll, lottery economics, property or lease terms, traffic access, competition, capital expenditures, working capital, and the cash flow remaining after normalized expenses and debt service.
